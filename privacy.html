HitShort Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HitShort handles information when users create short-form videos, connect supported social media accounts, review content, and choose to publish content to those accounts.

1. Information We Process

  • Connected account information: identifiers and basic profile or channel information returned by supported platforms after you authorize a connection.
  • Authorization information: access tokens, refresh tokens, authorization codes, scopes, and related connection metadata needed to maintain a connection and perform actions you request.
  • Content and publishing information: videos and related titles, captions, descriptions, cover selections, privacy settings, publishing status, and other metadata needed to prepare or publish content.
  • Service information: technical and operational information needed to run HitShort, diagnose errors, maintain task status, and protect the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide HitShort features requested by users, including connecting accounts, identifying the connected account, preparing videos for review, publishing user-approved content, maintaining connection status, troubleshooting failures, and protecting the service.

3. User Control and Publishing

Connecting an account does not by itself authorize HitShort to publish arbitrary content. HitShort is designed so that users review and approve content before a publishing action is initiated. Users can disconnect supported accounts or revoke access through the applicable platform settings.

4. Third-Party Platforms

HitShort integrates with third-party platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. When you connect or use those services, information may be exchanged with the relevant platform as necessary to provide the requested feature. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Sharing and Sale of Personal Information

HitShort does not sell personal information. Information is shared only as needed to provide requested functionality, operate supporting infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service and its users.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide HitShort, maintain account connections and operational records, resolve issues, or meet applicable legal requirements. When a connected account is disconnected or access is revoked, related authorization information is no longer used for new platform actions and may be deleted or invalidated as appropriate. Users may request deletion of personal information by contacting us at the address below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect account credentials, authorization tokens, content, and service data. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

HitShort is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the connected social media platforms or applicable law.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of HitShort. You may also revoke a platform connection through the relevant platform's account settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as HitShort features, integrations, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact ssyunsu@gmail.com.
